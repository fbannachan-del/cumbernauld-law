Who we are

The Cumbernauld Law Practice, 30 Ettrick Walk, Cumbernauld, G67 1NE, is the controller of the personal information collected through this website. You can phone us on 01236 731911.

What we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we collect the details you give us: your name, your contact details and your message. Please do not include confidential or sensitive information in your message.

Why we use it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to provide legal services to you.

Our legal basis

We use your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and for our legitimate interests in dealing with enquiries.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that are not taken forward are deleted within 12 months. If you become a client, we keep your file in line with Law Society of Scotland guidance.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct it, to ask us to erase it or restrict how we use it, to object to our use of it and, in some circumstances, to ask for it to be transferred to you.

Concerns

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first, by phone or at the address above. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
