Two long-standing Cumbernauld firms, one practice.

Fourteen people in one office on Ettrick Walk, so the person who answers the phone will know your matter.

The Cumbernauld Law Practice was formed on 1st May 2017 through the combination of the two longstanding Cumbernauld based legal practices of Barrowman & Partners and Ian S Smart & Company. The firm offers a full general practice legal service including, if required, both civil and criminal legal aid.

More about the practice

Six solicitors and trainees work alongside a support team of eight. We handle most matters in-house, and where a case needs a specialist we refer it to another firm at no cost to you.

Whether it is a divorce, a court date, a house purchase or a will, you will deal with people who live and work here and who know how things are done locally. We are members of the Law Society of Scotland, and we also act for clients in Airdrie, Coatbridge, Falkirk, Glasgow and Hamilton.
